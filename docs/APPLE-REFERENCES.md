# Apple instruction sources

Accessed 6 September 2026.

- Account deletion: https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/
- Cancelling a subscription: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428
- Refund requests: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223
- Standard EULA: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
- App privacy requirements: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy

Apple's documented requirements are platform guidance. The product-specific data categories and account-settings wording are the proposed website copy, not facts extracted from a Fontly build. Removing an app or account does not cancel Apple billing. An ordinary app with account creation needs an in-app account-deletion initiation path; an email link is supplementary help, not a substitute.
