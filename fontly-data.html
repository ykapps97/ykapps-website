Delete your account

Open Fontly's settings, choose the account-deletion option and follow the confirmation steps. This removes your account and associated personal data, except information we are legally required to keep.

Files you have exported are separate. Delete those files from the device or service where you saved them.

Cancel a subscription

Deleting Fontly or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Fontly > Cancel Subscription.

Need help with your data?

Email hello@ykapps.ist to request access, correction or deletion of your information. Include the account email or identifier associated with your request. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
