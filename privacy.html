Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

YK Apps Yazılım Bilgi İşlem Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. ("YK Apps", "we", "us", or "our") builds and publishes mobile applications. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

It applies to our website at ykapps.ist and to every mobile application we publish, unless a specific app displays its own supplementary notice.

What we collect

We collect only what an app or the website needs to function. Not every category below applies to every app. Each app's App Store and Google Play listing carries a data disclosure specific to that app, and that disclosure governs for that app.

Device and technical data. Device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, anonymous device or installation identifiers, and crash and diagnostic reports. Used to keep the app working and to fix defects.

Usage data. Which screens are opened, which features are used, and how often. Aggregated and used to decide what to improve. We do not use it to build a profile of you as an individual.

Purchase and subscription data. Whether you hold an active subscription, which product you bought, and the status of that subscription. This comes to us from Apple and Google. We never receive your card number, bank details, or billing address. Payment is handled entirely by the store you bought from.

Content you provide to an app. Text, images, audio, or files you choose to submit so the app can do what you asked. What happens to that content is set out in the section on content processing below.

Device permissions. Some apps request access to the camera, photo library, microphone, notifications, or similar. Each app requests only the permissions its features require, explains why at the moment of asking, and continues to work in a reduced form if you decline. You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings.

Correspondence. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply and so we can recognise a follow-up.

Website data. ykapps.ist serves static pages and collects only the technical data needed to deliver them, such as IP address and request time in standard server logs, retained briefly for security and reliability. The site does not set advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites.

How we handle content you submit

If an app processes content you provide, we do so only to produce the result you requested.

We do not sell your content. We do not use it to build advertising profiles. We do not use identifiable user content to train machine learning models. If that ever changes for a particular app, that app will ask for your explicit consent first, and you will be able to decline and keep using it.

Where processing happens on a third-party service, the content is transmitted for that purpose, processed, and deleted according to that provider's retention terms. Where processing can happen on your device, it does.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run our products, and only to the extent they need it. Each is bound by a contract that limits them to our instructions.

The categories of provider we rely on are:

  • App distribution and payment processing, namely Apple and Google
  • Crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Product analytics
  • Install attribution and marketing measurement
  • Subscription and entitlement management
  • Cloud hosting and storage
  • Email and customer support

A current list of the specific providers we use is available on request from hello@ykapps.ist.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing partners for their own purposes.

We may disclose data where the law requires it, where we must defend a legal claim, or where a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition occurs. In the last case you will be told before your data becomes subject to a different policy.

International transfers

We operate from Türkiye and use service providers located outside Türkiye and outside the European Economic Area. Where we transfer personal data abroad, we do so on a lawful basis, using standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard, and in accordance with Article 9 of KVKK.

How long we keep data

  • Diagnostic and crash data: up to 12 months
  • Aggregate usage data: retained in aggregate, no longer identifiable
  • Subscription and purchase records: for the period required by Turkish tax and commercial law
  • Support correspondence: up to 24 months from the last message
  • Content submitted for processing: deleted once the result is delivered, unless the app explicitly stores it for you

When a retention period ends, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11, and under the GDPR where it applies to you, you may:

  • Ask whether we hold data about you, and obtain a copy
  • Ask for inaccurate data to be corrected
  • Ask for your data to be deleted
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Ask us to restrict processing while a dispute is resolved
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where consent was the basis
  • Lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu) or your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these, email hello@ykapps.ist with the subject line "Data request". We respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that we do not disclose your data to someone else.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Where an app is rated for a younger audience, the additional protections required by that rating apply and are described in the app's store listing. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to hello@ykapps.ist and we will delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, restrict internal access to what each person needs, and review our providers' security posture. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal data and poses a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authority within the timeframes the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our products or the law change. The "last updated" date above always reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email before they take effect.
