How this website handles information and how to contact us about privacy.

This notice covers the YK Apps website and messages you send to us. Each native app has its own privacy policy, available from its app page.

Using this website

Our hosting service receives the technical information needed to deliver a page, such as your IP address, browser information, request time and requested URL. Technical logs support delivery, troubleshooting and security.

The website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, load tracking pixels or use third-party fonts. Its images, icons, scripts and styles are served with the website.

Messages and support

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments. We use them to respond and help with your request. Our email provider processes correspondence on our behalf.

The support form prepares a message in your own email app. It does not send your answers to a website server. We receive the message only when you send it. Copy buttons access your clipboard only when you choose to use them.

The web sketchpad

The lettering demo keeps drawing strokes in the memory of your current browser tab. Drawings are not uploaded or saved in browser storage. Reloading or closing the page clears them. Saving a drawing creates a PNG file on your device.

Why we use information

We use information to deliver the website, respond to requests and protect our services. Depending on the purpose, processing is based on providing a requested service, our legitimate interests in support and security, a legal obligation, or consent where required. Consent can be withdrawn.

Service providers and external links

Hosting and email providers process the information necessary to provide their services. We may disclose information when required by law or to handle a legal claim. Where information is processed outside your country, applicable data-transfer safeguards apply.

Links to Apple and other external services are governed by those services' privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep support information for as long as needed to handle the request, related follow-up and any legal obligation. Technical logs are retained for delivery and security purposes according to the hosting service's settings. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority.

Contact hello@ykapps.ist. We may ask for limited information to verify that a request concerns you. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Changes

We update this notice when the website's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
