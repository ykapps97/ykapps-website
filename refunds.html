Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for purchases relating to applications published by YK Apps Yazılım Bilgi İşlem Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. ("YK Apps", "we", "us", or "our").

Start here: where did you buy?

The answer depends entirely on where the purchase was made, because that determines who took your money.

Where you boughtWho handles the refund
Inside an app on iPhone or iPadApple
Inside an app on AndroidGoogle
Directly from us on ykapps.istYK Apps

If you bought inside one of our apps, the payment went to Apple or Google, not to us. We never receive your card details and we have no ability to charge or refund your payment method. We can help you with the process, but the decision is theirs.

Purchases made through the Apple App Store

Apple processes all payments made inside our iOS apps and handles refunds under its own policy.

To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase, and choose the option to request a refund. You can also submit a request from your device in Settings, or through Apple Support.

To cancel a subscription, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then the subscription, then Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Cancelling stops future charges and keeps your access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Apple decides refund requests. We cannot approve, reverse, or expedite that decision. If Apple declines and you believe the outcome is wrong, write to us at support@ykapps.ist and we will tell you what, if anything, we can do.

Purchases made through Google Play

Google processes all payments made inside our Android apps.

To request a refund, use play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, find the order, and select the refund option. Google generally handles requests made within 48 hours of purchase automatically, and reviews later requests case by case.

To cancel a subscription, open the Google Play Store app, go to Subscriptions in your account, select the subscription, and cancel. Cancel before the renewal date. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

As with Apple, the decision is Google's.

Purchases made directly from us

If we ever sell a subscription or product directly through ykapps.ist rather than through an app store, the following applies to that purchase.

You may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the transaction date.

Requests are reviewed individually. Approved requests are refunded in full to the original payment method. We do not issue partial refunds against a single transaction. Submitting a request inside the 14-day window does not by itself guarantee approval.

Refunds apply only to the transaction the request concerns. We do not refund earlier billing periods retroactively.

Approved refunds are issued within 14 calendar days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take several further business days to show it on your statement.

To request one, email support@ykapps.ist with the email address used for the purchase and your order or transaction number. We may ask for further detail to verify the purchase.

Cancelling is not the same as refunding

Cancelling ends future billing. It does not return money already charged.

If you cancel partway through a paid period, you keep access until that period ends and you are not charged again. If you also want the current period refunded, submit a refund request through the relevant channel above.

Free trials

If a free trial is offered, cancel before it ends and you are not charged. If the trial converts to a paid subscription because it was not cancelled in time, the refund routes above apply.

If something is broken

If an app is not working as it should, contact support@ykapps.ist before requesting a refund. Many problems are fixable quickly, and we would rather fix them.

Contacting us does not pause any refund deadline. If you are close to a time limit, submit the refund request as well.

Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit rights you have under mandatory law.

If you are in Türkiye, your rights under the Law on Consumer Protection No. 6502 and the Regulation on Distance Contracts apply. Note that the right of withdrawal for digital content delivered immediately, with your prior consent, is subject to the exceptions set out in that legislation.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, your statutory rights, including the right of withdrawal and remedies for non-conforming digital content, apply with the same qualification for immediately delivered content.

Where the law gives you stronger rights than this policy, the law wins.

Payment disputes

If you are unhappy with a purchase, contact us or the relevant store before starting a chargeback with your bank. A chargeback on an app store purchase is decided by Apple or Google and may result in the loss of access to purchased content across their platform, which is usually a worse outcome than a normal refund request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version in effect on the date of your transaction governs that transaction.
