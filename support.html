Support

We answer every message

Get help with an app

If something isn't working, or you have a question about any of our apps, email us at support@ykapps.ist. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

To help us solve your issue faster, please include:

  • The name of the app
  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18)
  • A short description of the problem, and a screenshot if possible

Subscriptions & refunds

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play, not by us directly. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your device's subscription settings, and request refunds through Apple or Google. Our Refund Policy has step-by-step instructions. If you're stuck, write to us and we'll point you in the right direction.

Everything else

For general, business, or legal enquiries, contact hello@ykapps.ist.
